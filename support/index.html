Help & Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to help@abidetroit.com. JPLAY is published by Abid Design LLC.

Restore a purchase

Bought JPLAY Pro before, or on another iPhone with the same Apple Account? Open JPLAY and tap Restore Purchases on the JPLAY Pro screen, or in Settings › Restore Purchases.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions › JPLAY. Subscribers can also tap Manage Subscription in JPLAY’s Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Daily reminders

Turn the daily reminder on or off and choose its time in JPLAY’s Settings › Reminders. If iOS has blocked JPLAY’s notifications, allow them in Settings › Notifications › JPLAY.

Your progress and privacy

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone; JPLAY has no accounts and collects no personal information. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Read the Privacy Policy.

Replay the introduction

Settings › Onboarding plays the introduction again. Your progress stays saved.
