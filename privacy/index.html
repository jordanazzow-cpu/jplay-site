JPLAY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Publisher: Abid Design LLC (“we”, “us”) · Contact: help@abidetroit.com

Summary

JPLAY does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your learning progress and settings stay on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple.

What JPLAY keeps on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Learning progress: lessons and levels completed, your Journey position and rank, placement results from the introduction, practice results, SmartCards review history and schedule, collected words, streak days and your daily goal.
  • Settings: theme, voice, sound, haptics, SmartCards preferences, reminder preferences and whether you finished the introduction.

This data may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your own backup settings.

What JPLAY does not collect

We do not collect or receive your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera input, device or advertising identifiers, or information about how you use the app. JPLAY does not send your data to any server of ours (we do not run one) and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. JPLAY is built with Capacitor, an open-source app framework; it runs inside the app and sends nothing to us or to its makers.

Purchases

JPLAY is free to download. After the introduction, continuing requires JPLAY Pro, sold as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or a one-time lifetime purchase through Apple’s In-App Purchase.

  • Apple processes every payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • To unlock the app, JPLAY asks Apple’s StoreKit on your iPhone whether your Apple Account owns JPLAY Pro. That check runs between your device and Apple; no purchase record is sent to us.
  • Apple handles purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Apple Media Services Terms and Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Reminders

JPLAY can send one daily reminder. It is off until you turn it on, either in JPLAY’s Settings or from a short explanation JPLAY may show after you have played, which continues to the iOS permission prompt. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in JPLAY’s Settings or in the iOS Settings app (Notifications › JPLAY).

Links to websites

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Help links open in Safari. Those pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics or tracking to them.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports. They come from Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and do not identify you.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share or give your personal information to anyone, because we do not collect it. No third party receives data about you from JPLAY.

Children

JPLAY is a general-audience learning app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete the app. Deleting JPLAY deletes all of it (copies inside your own device backups follow your backup settings). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete; you can still write to us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If JPLAY’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the App Store.
